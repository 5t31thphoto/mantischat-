MantisChat

Chat with Mantis Watches over ESP-NOW, and draw on a whiteboard everyone in range shares.

Flash your Core2

Plug the M5Stack Core2 in over USB-C and use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. If no port shows up, install the CP210x or CH9102 USB driver and try again.

Your browser can't flash over USB. Open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge, or download the binary below. Flashing needs a secure (https) page. Download merged .bin

The merged image flashes at offset 0x0:
esptool.py --chip esp32 write_flash 0x0 mantischat-core2-merged.bin
Build {{SHORT_SHA}}, {{BUILD_DATE}}.

Chat

Group chat on channel 0 and DMs to anyone under People. Speaks MantisNow v1 and the older MNW1 watch chat.

Whiteboard

Every stroke appears live on every board nearby. Undo, erase and clear only ever remove your own ink.

Buttons

A home, B chat or keyboard, C whiteboard. Tap your callsign on the home screen to change it.
